# Cross-PC import and robot.ini controls

Read the [experiment report](../../../Evidence/PCImportsRobotIni_2026-09-30/REPORT.md) first, then [manifest](../../../Evidence/PCImportsRobotIni_2026-09-30/manifest.json) and [native analysis](../../../Evidence/PCImportsRobotIni_2026-09-30/native_analysis.json). `IPCPROV.KL` supplies three typed identity routines. `IPCI.KL`, `IPCR.KL`, and `IPCB.KL` declare `FROM IPCPROV` and call those routines with INTEGER, REAL, and BOOLEAN compact locals. `BASI.KL`, `BASR.KL`, and `BASB.KL` hold the same local storage without an external declaration. `IPCDECL.KL` declares but never calls the provider routine; `IPCNODEC.KL` calls it without a declaration.

Each source is compiled in isolated scratch directories under a full V8.33 `robot.ini`, no `robot.ini` with `/ver V8.33-1`, no file or version option, or a deliberately incomplete version-only `robot.ini`, with/without the generated provider PC as indexed in the manifest. Accepted V8.33 outputs are under `PC/V8.33/PCImportConfig`; no-option outputs on this computer are V9.40 and are under `PC/V9.40/PCImportConfig`. Never pool those binaries as one versioned corpus. The [reproduction scripts](../../../Evidence/PCImportsRobotIni_2026-09-30/scripts/) and [compiler logs](../../../Evidence/PCImportsRobotIni_2026-09-30/compiler_logs/) preserve the exact setup and diagnostics.
